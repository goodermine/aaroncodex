# Handoff — 26 Sep 2026 session: no warm-up, post-birthday vocal fatigue

## Context (from Aaron directly)

Aaron's 50th birthday party was the night before this session (25 Sep), and
"we did hundreds of songs singing" that night. The following night (26 Sep,
Bonny View and Redcliffe Tavern), Aaron and Rilda went straight from dinner
into karaoke with **no vocal warm-up at all** — a spontaneous, unplanned
session, not a normal drilled outing.

Rilda's own words, given directly to Aaron right after singing "Dancing
Queen": she said "we should have warmed up," and that she "couldn't do any
belting at all" and had to sing noticeably softer than usual.

This is documented here — attributed to the singers, not asserted as a
diagnosis by the analysis system — so a future reading of this session's
scores doesn't misread a real, explained dip as an unexplained technical
regression. Same pattern as `HANDOFF_SESSION_2026-09-06.md` §6 and
`HANDOFF_ALL_THAT_SHE_WANTS_SEP16220_CONTEXT.md`.

## What this is not

This context does not excuse or explain away every number in this session —
several components (e.g. phrase control, breath support on individual takes)
may have independent, unrelated causes. It is provided so a reader comparing
this session against these singers' established history has the same
information the singers had at the time, rather than reading a session-wide
dip as a mystery.
