# Coined / invented and short abstract brand names for "VOX Analysis" (screened Oct 2026)

Scope: coined or short abstract names only. No names built on the bare word "Vox". All checks were run on **2026-10-14**. They are web and registry signals, **not legal clearance**. No live USPTO, EUIPO or IP Australia database query was possible: their search UIs are JavaScript apps that the fetch tool cannot drive. Trademark evidence therefore comes from Justia and uspto.report pages found by search, and is incomplete.

**Domain method.** These are direct registry RDAP lookups, which are authoritative for whether a name is *registered*. They do not show whether a domain is for sale.
- .com: `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`
- .app: `https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/<name>.app`
- .ai: `https://rdap.identitydigital.services/rdap/domain/<name>.ai`. This is the .ai RDAP server listed in the [IANA RDAP bootstrap file](https://data.iana.org/rdap/dns.json).
- HTTP 404 means **not registered** at the time of the check. HTTP 200 means registered.
- For registered .coms, the registrar and nameservers were read from the RDAP record. Nameservers at afternic.com, brandbucket.com or above.com mean the domain is parked or listed for sale.

## Which coined names feel credible (scientific) yet warm enough for singers?

### Takeaway
Two kinds of name balance "scientific" and "warm" best:
- **Singing-insider root plus a measurement suffix:** **Tessimetry** (tessitura + -metry) and **Cantometry** (canto + -metry).
- **Voice root plus a technical pun:** **Vocent** (voice + *cent*, the 1/100-semitone unit the engine actually measures in).

Warmer but less "scientific" options are **Intonara**, **Voceline** and **Melodri**. The "-metry" names read as rigorous but are a poor fit as a *master* brand over a Polish tool and a pitch monitor. Vocent or a short abstract name stretches across the suite more easily.

### Cited Findings

**Root semantics**
- "Cantometrics" is an established academic term for measuring singing style. Alan Lomax and Victor Grauer built it in the 1960s; it rates 37 style factors on five-point scales — [Wikipedia: Cantometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantometrics); [Cultural Equity: Song Style Analysis](https://www.culturalequity.org/research/song-style).
  - The Global Jukebox release codes 5,776 songs from 1,026 societies, and the method "drew considerable criticism" — [PMC9629617](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9629617).
  - So "Canto-metry" carries real scientific heritage, but also a known, contested academic association.
- "Vocometry" is already a vocology term. NYU runs a course called "Vocometry: Measuring and Analyzing the Voice" — [NYU SPS](https://sps.nyu.edu/professional-pathways/courses/COMM1-CE1003-vocometry-measuring-and-analyzing-the-voice.html). A search for "Vocimetry/Vocimetric" found no uses, but it is one vowel away from that descriptive term — [search result set, same NYU page](https://www.sps.nyu.edu/courses/COMM1-CE1003-vocometry-measuring-and-analyzing-the-voice.html).

**Competitor scoring language**
- Singing apps already sell 1–10 vocal ratings across parameters such as "pitch accuracy, tone quality, breath control, rhythm, and vocal projection" — [ScreenApp 2026 voice rating tools roundup](https://screenapp.io/de/blog/best-voice-rating-tools-2026) (self-interested publisher).
- PitchCheck scores pitch accuracy out of 100 — [App Store: PitchCheck](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6760937562).
- Category vocabulary is drifting toward "score/rate/check" names. A "-metry"/"cent" coinage stands apart from it.

### Inferences
- **Tessimetry** speaks directly to trained singers, since tessitura is the comfortable singing range. It reads like a measurement discipline, matching the "scientific-grade measurement with a verdict on top" positioning. Casual karaoke users may not know the root, but they can pronounce it.
- **Vocent** is the most "two-level" name. To a layperson it reads as "voice + accent/scent". To a musician or engineer, the *cents* pun signals precision. It also works as a master brand: Vocent Analyse / Vocent Polish / Vocent Monitor.
- **Cantometry** is the most overtly scientific. Its closeness to Lomax's Cantometrics could be pitched as lineage, but it also weakens distinctiveness (see the next section).
- **Intonara** and **Voceline** are warm and Italianate but read less like "measurement". **Melodri** and **Cantrel** are abstract and neutral; they would need brand building to carry meaning.

### Gaps
- No consumer testing was done. Warmth and credibility judgements above are the researcher's assessment, not data.

## Which are genuinely free of conflicts in software/music/voice classes?

### Takeaway
On web and registry signals, the cleanest options are:
- **Tessimetry**, **Melodri**, **Singalyst** and **Cantometry**: .com, .app and .ai all unregistered, and no conflicting brand found. Cantometry still carries the Lomax descriptiveness issue.
- **Vocent**: no active conflict found, and the .com is **buyable on Afternic for US$3,988**. There is a dormant 2000s voice-software prior user.

Reject **VocalIQ** (Apple), **Tessiva** (an active singer's name and site) and **Vocelo** (too close to the Voloco singing app). Treat **Intonara** and **Verivoce** with caution: both .coms were freshly registered in April 2026.

### Inferences
- **Best ownability today: Tessimetry.** It is coined, has no conflicts found, and is free in all three TLDs. Next are Melodri and Voceline (also all-free), then Cantometry (all-free but with a descriptiveness concern).
- **Best name that can be bought: Vocent.** It costs about US$4k for the .com and has a strong meaning. The prior user appears dormant and was acquired, but its old trademark history must be cleared at USPTO and IP Australia first.
- "-metry" coinages are free because they are long (10 letters). Short (5–7 letter) Italianate blends are almost all registered in .com and often .app. The short-name space is exhausted, so a short name will cost money or use .app/.ai.
- Two candidates (Intonara, Verivoce) had their .com registered in April 2026. That suggests other people are now mining the same voice/voce/intonation roots, so the window on any free name is short. Registering the chosen .com/.app/.ai immediately is cheap insurance.

### Gaps
- **No primary trademark-register search was completed** for any name. USPTO tmsearch, EUIPO eSearch and IP Australia ATMOSS/Search are JavaScript apps the fetch tool could not run. Each shortlisted name needs a manual check in Classes 9, 41 and 42 in AU, US and EU.
- The BrandBucket price for vocelo.com was not retrievable (HTTP 403). The Above.com price for cantrel.com was not checked.
- App Store / Google Play searches were done only indirectly, through web search. No exact-name app was found for Tessimetry, Cantometry, Melodri, Voceline, Singalyst or Vocent, but store-side search was not run directly.
- Meanings in other languages were checked only from the researcher's own knowledge (e.g. "você" = "you" in Portuguese). No offensive meanings are known, but no native-speaker check was done.
- I could not tell whether Vocent Solutions' marks are fully dead or were assigned to its acquirer.

## Existing coined names in the space to avoid (verification)

### Takeaway
Confirmed existing:
- **Vanido** (defunct ~2022), **Vocaberry** (live), **Singeo** (Musora, live), **Yousician** (live)
- **Voxal** (NCH voice changer) and **VocAlign** (Synchro Arts alignment plug-in)
- **Phonetiq**, which exists but is a Siri-contacts utility, not a singing app
- Also found: **Voloco**, **Melodics**, **Vocular**, **Tonara** (defunct), **VocalIQ** (Apple), **Vocalina** and **Vocoflex**

**Vocalyzer** could not be verified. **Voxengo** was not verified in this pass, but it is out of scope anyway (Vox-root).

### Cited Findings
**Singing apps and services**
- **Vanido**: a free singing app with real-time pitch detection. AlternativeTo lists it as discontinued around 2022 — [Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/vanido-can-a-free-app-help-you-learn-to-sing-better); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/vanido/about).
- **Vocaberry**: "Learn to sing with Vocaberry", live on the App Store, gives pitch-accuracy feedback — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1333485458).
- **Singeo**: Musora's online singing-lessons platform (sister of Pianote and Drumeo), added Aug 2021 — [MusicRadar review](https://www.musicradar.com/reviews/singeo-review); [American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/singeo-review/).
- **Yousician**: Helsinki music-learning company, founded 2010 (formerly Ovelin), also makes GuitarTuna — [Builtin](https://builtin.com/company/yousician); [Motherbase](https://my.motherbase.ai/company/79064-yousician).
- **Voloco**: singing/rapping recording app with auto-tune, claiming 50M downloads, by Resonant Cavity Inc. — [AppleVis](https://applevis.com/comment/161810); [Voloco help](https://help.voloco.com/hc/en-us/articles/24407619982871-How-do-I-find-the-tracks-and-beats-I-like).
- **Melodics**: music practice app — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melodics).
- **Vocular**: iOS vocal-analysis app that compares the user's voice with 150+ celebrities — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1080181813).
- **Tonara**: music practice app (defunct; shutdown date conflicts between 2023 and 2024) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonara_(company)).

**Voice and audio software**
- **Voxal**: NCH Software's voice changer — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/voxal-voice-changer/about).
- **VocAlign**: Synchro Arts' audio-alignment plug-in — [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/news/vocalign-vst-format).
- **Phonetiq**: Qloud LTD's iPhone app that adds phonetic spellings to contacts for Siri/CarPlay, not a singing app — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/phonetiq/id6743021637).
- **VocalIQ**: Apple-acquired speech AI — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/apple-acquires-deep-learning-startup-vocaliq-to-make-siri-smarter).
- **Vocalina**: Korean singing TTS, discontinued — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocalina).
- **Vocoflex**: Dreamtonics AI voice-transformation plug-in — [Making a Scene](https://www.makingascene.org/tag/vocoder/).

**Trademarks**
- **VOCANTO** is a registered US mark of Lucas-Nülle GmbH — [Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/863/49/vocanto-86349996.html). Avoid voc+canto blends that look like it, e.g. "Vocanto" or "Vocantra".

### Inferences
- The crowded zones are:
  - **Voc-/Vocal-** prefixes: Vocaberry, Vocular, VocalIQ, VocAlign, Vocalina, Vocoflex, Voloco
  - **Sing-** prefixes: Singeo, Sing Sharp, Singing Carrots
  - **Melod-** prefixes: Melodics
- The least crowded roots are **tessitura (Tess-)**, **-metry**, and the **cent** pun. That supports Tessimetry and Vocent as the most differentiated shortlist.
- Any "Voce-" or "Voc-" name sits closer to this crowd than a Canto/Tessi/-metry name. It needs a more careful likelihood-of-confusion check in Class 9.

### Gaps
- **Vocalyzer**: no product of that name surfaced in search, so I could not confirm it exists.
- **Voxengo**: not verified in this pass. It is Vox-rooted, so it is out of this file's scope.
