Deliver every analysis in two parts, in this order. Aaron reads results on his
phone.

1. **Plain English first.** Explain the whole result without jargon:
   - Open with the headline /10 (rule 5's lead number, the other beside it),
     confidence, and what 10 means (a typical pro).
   - Then cover each component in everyday words, how notes start, the
     words-vs-notes wobbles, range and voice, timestamps to listen back to,
     the practice plan, and what the engine can't tell.
   - Translate units: 100 cents = one semitone, "the gap between two
     neighbouring piano keys". Say "three-quarters of a semitone", not
     "76 cents".
   - Mark any suggestion of your own (for example, a key change) as yours,
     not the engine's.
2. **Then the full technical results**, complete and unedited in content
   (CLAUDE.md rules 6 and 8), shown as **formatted markdown, never inside a
   code block**:
   - `###` headings per section; every line as its own paragraph or bullet.
   - Component and metric rows as bullets.
   - Code blocks don't wrap on a phone and force side-scrolling.

Applies to every singer's results delivered in chat.
