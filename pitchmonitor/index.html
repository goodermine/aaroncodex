Real-time pitch

Sing or play into the mic and watch your pitch trace across the note grid. Tap to allow the microphone.

Settings

Tonic and in-scale notes are tinted on the grid. Chromatic shows every semitone equally.

Spectrum view (top-right SPEC) shows a live scrolling spectrogram with your pitch and its harmonics marked, and the 2–4 kHz "ring" band highlighted.

Targets are drawn at random between these. Default A3–F4 — the working range; raise the top as clean entries become routine.

Pitch detection is monophonic — one voice or instrument at a time. Two notes or a chord won't read correctly. Recording captures the pitch trace for playback.
