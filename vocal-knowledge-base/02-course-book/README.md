# Course Book

This folder holds **three separate long-form works**. They are not editions of each other.

The two books were verified by paragraph-level comparison: **zero shared text.** They cover
overlapping topics in different words, with different structures and different chapter
numbering. Keep them apart — conflating them is how the numbering confusion started.

---

## `four-machine-course/` — the training programme

**4 weeks · 2 sessions per week · ~5,200 words across two documents**

The most directly practical material in the library, and the newest.

- **`the-four-machine-course.md`** — the programme itself. Frames the voice as four stacked
  systems (breath → cords → vocal tract → nervous system) and trains them in the order they
  physically stack. One week per machine. Carries the diagnostic table that turns a failing
  note into a specific fix, and the golden rule: *never repeat a failing note louder.*
- **`formants-deep-and-wide.md`** — the acoustics theory behind Machine 3 (the filter).
  Source–filter independence, the F1 ceiling, formant tuning strategies, twang versus
  nasality. Also the clearest standalone explanation of resonance in this library.

Start here if you want something to actually train from this week.

## `singing-fundamentals-compiled/` — the reference encyclopedia

**21 chapters · ~71,000 words · single file**

*Singing Fundamentals — From Beginner to Advanced.* Built on a "Seven Pillars" spine
(posture, breath, pitch, tone, diction, rhythm, vocal health), running from foundations
through performance skills to teaching methods. Includes a short A–Z glossary appendix
and two images.

Structure: three PARTs → nine SECTIONs → 21 chapters, each closing with a Chapter Reflection.

Two repairs were made to this file: Chapters 10 and 11 were out of sequence in the source
and have been moved into place, and the heading hierarchy has been normalised (chapters had
been applied at `##`, `###` and `####` depth at random). It now runs 1→21 with a consistent
outline.
