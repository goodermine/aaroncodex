Aaron's M2/mixed voice zone is his strongest technical range. Songs with core
tessitura A3–B4 and passaggio around D4 consistently score highest (Oh What A
Night 9.5 capture-fair, Kung Fu Fighting 9.4, Do Wah Diddy 9.2). Songs that
sit lower and force register transitions (Rebel Yell core E3–G4, passaggio C#4)
trigger Pitch Slider and score significantly lower. Aaron is "not used to"
hearing himself in M2/mixed and doesn't think it sounds great — but it's where
the data says he's most precise.
