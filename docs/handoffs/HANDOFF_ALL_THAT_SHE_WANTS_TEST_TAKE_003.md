## Coaching note

The changed approach improves pitch arrival, phrase shaping and dynamic
contrast, but the notes move more after landing. Keep the test take's clearer
arrival and expressive phrasing while reducing sustained-note drift below 76
cents at approximately 80% intensity.

Primary drill: **Interval Leaps — 1–3 and 1–5**. Hear the destination first,
sing slowly on “mum”, hold it for two seconds, then transfer it to one song
phrase. Cue: **“Hear it first, then sing it. Don’t fish for the note.”**
