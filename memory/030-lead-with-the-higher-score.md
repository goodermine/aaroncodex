When giving an analysis, always lead with the HIGHER of the overall and
capture-fair scores, on any capture (studio, live, tavern, phone, room), and give
the other one beside it. This replaces "studio → overall, live → capture-fair",
which on Rilda's live On The Radio (14 Oct 2026) led with 7.2 capture-fair when
the overall was 7.9. Written into CLAUDE.md rule 5. `report_builder` prints the
`Lead with:` line for you.
