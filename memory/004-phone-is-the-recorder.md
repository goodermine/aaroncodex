Aaron has two recording paths: (1) phone for raw/live takes — conflicts with
reading lyrics, so Rilda's phone records + paper cue card for lyrics;
(2) TazCam mixer for cleaner but dynamics-compressed captures (11 dB phrase
spread vs 44 dB on phone). For clean stems at home: backing track through
headphones (not speakers), phone records just the vocal. Know which path a
take came from before interpreting component scores — TazCam squashes dynamics.
