The TazCam mixer compresses vocal dynamics. Oh What A Night TazCam take showed
11 dB phrase-level dynamic spread vs 44 dB on the phone recording of the same
song, same session. Dynamics score (7.53 TazCam vs 8.81 phone) reflects the
recording chain, not the singing. When reviewing TazCam recordings, dynamics is
a capture artefact — use capture-fair scoring and don't coach on dynamics from
mixer recordings.

> evidence: comparative analysis of take-001 (TazCam MP3, 11 dB spread) vs take-002 (phone M4A, 44 dB spread) — session of 2026-08-05
