## The two findings the whole campaign hangs on

**1. The competition killer is the pressure response, not skill.**
Aaron's semi loss self-diagnosis ("nerves / pushed too hard") matches what two
independent systems measured: the old profile ("loud gear too early, slides
into notes") and VOX this week (Brighton over-push when monitoring vanished;
42–49% scooped onsets). Under pressure he defaults to power. The counter-cue,
proven at Brighton KFF (9.3 sung with zero self-monitoring): **"keep one gear
in reserve — normal support, the mic does the loudness."**

**2. In competition there is no song two.**
The measured warm-into-the-night curve (intonation 6.0 → 7.0 → 10.0 across
three songs at Bramble; best takes always mid-set) means Aaron's song #1 is
routinely 1–3 points below his song #3. A competition night is **one song**.
He must arrive in song-three condition: full home warm-up + car singing +
quiet sing-along at the venue before his slot. This alone is worth more than
any drill.

## The song ladder (different song each round)

| Round | Song | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Heat | **Kung Fu Fighting** | 9.0 & 9.3 room-fair live this week; intonation 10.0, phrase control 10.0 — judge-clean AND crowd-igniting; survives bad monitoring |
| Quarter-final | **Pressure Down** | signature, 9.3, drilled 9×, Aussie crowd + real musical substance |
| Semi-final | **Showpiece A** (data decides by Oct — see below) | the judges'-ears round he keeps losing |
| Grand Final | **Showpiece B** (the other candidate) | leave nothing behind |
| Backup | Oh What A Night (8.9) / One (8.7) | if a ladder song is burned or the rules surprise us |

**Showpiece candidates — both built weekly Aug–Sep, warm-ups decide:**
- **Reasons** — climbing (7.7 → 8.3 in a week); ballad that shows judges range
  + storytelling; weak spots (opening settle, passaggio floats) are already the
  drill plan.
- **You're the Voice** — the Aussie anthem, highest ceiling, untested;
  sustain-heavy so it matures exactly as held-note stability does.
- Decision rule: whichever trends higher across the weekly takes sings the
  semi; the other (if ready) sings the final. Not ready ≠ sung.

## Weekly rhythm (Aug 1 → Oct 7, ~10 weeks)

1. **One recorded take each of Reasons and You're the Voice per week** (home,
   supply-your-own-backing) → two trend lines into October.
2. **Pressure Down + Kung Fu Fighting maintained fortnightly** — keep the
   bankers warm, don't over-drill the plateau into them.
3. **Friday karaoke nights = pressure inoculation.** Sing a ladder song each
   week in a loud room; the goal is executing "one gear in reserve" under
   adrenaline, not the score.
4. **Daily micro-work stays on the two measured gaps:** entrance planting
   ("see it, land it, stay on it") and sustains (Messa di Voce — still first,
   shape second), plus rib-wide-to-the-last-word until it survives fatigue
   (it vanished when tired at Brighton — not yet automatic).
5. **You Sexy Thing and other plateaued songs are parked** — fun material
   only, no measurement, revisit after the competition.

## Competition-night protocol (every round)

- Full warm-up at home + car + quiet venue sing-along: walk on in song-three
  condition.
- One cue only on stage: **"one gear in reserve — let the chorus pull it out
  of me."** (Under nerves, one cue survives; three don't.)
- No foldback assumed: pitch from the backing's bass/keys; cup one ear on
  exposed sustains.
- Record every round; the take gets analysed and tagged live like any other.

- **By Oct 1:** showpiece decision data-ready; ladder locked pending warm-ups.
- **Oct 7/14 warm-ups:** sing the semi candidates live, recorded — final call.
- **Nov:** ladder locked, weekly maintenance only, protect the voice.
