Aaron struggles with conscious pitch matching — even when he hears a target
tone, he tends to overshoot or undershoot rather than landing on it. His in-tune
singing happens when his voice "just goes there" rather than when he's actively
trying to match. This is the core training gap the onset trainer targets.

> evidence: "when I tried that app I have trouble even when I hear the tone to [match it]... I tend to be either way above it or way below it and then occasionally I sort of get it right and that's only because that's where my voice seems to be hitting" — session of 2026-08-04
